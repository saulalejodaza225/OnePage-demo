## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the conceptual thesis *"la página es la vela"* (the page is the sail)—positioning high-converting digital storefronts as precision-cut sails that harness commercial wind for businesses across Colombia and Latin America.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Modernism** with **Maritime Craftsmanship**:
- **Personality**: Resolute, artisanal, commercially sharp, authoritative, and welcoming.
- **Target Audience**: Ambitious founders, boutique business owners, and growing service companies seeking conversion-optimized, premium digital assets without agency friction.
- **Emotional Impact**: Generates the tactile sensation of heavy deckled paper stock, bespoke sea-charts, and modern computational precision. Conveys total technical reliability paired with warm Bogotá coffee-shop approachability.
- **Visual Texture**: Crisp paper hues (`#FBF7EF`), structural navy canvas (`#0B1D33`), gilded brass highlights (`#F4B63F`), and physical page-fold geometries.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic tactile surfaces with abyssal nautical blues and golden navigational instruments.

### Palette Architecture
- **Paper Canvas (`#FBF7EF`)**: Warm cream stock serving as the primary page surface, reducing eye fatigue and differentiating instantly from sterile digital white.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure parchment white dedicated strictly to interactive cards, modals, and pricing matrices.
- **Secondary Warm Tint (`#EFE5D2`)**: Grounding band tone used for architectural striping, inset blocks, and badge backings.
- **Structural Border (`#E6DCC8`)**: Crisp, low-contrast boundary tone designed to give paper-cut definition without harsh visual noise.
- **Midnight Nautical Navy (`#0B1D33`)**: Deep maritime navy used for hero bands, footer expanses, authoritative display headlines, and high-contrast text layers.
- **Supporting Ink (`#5B6778`)**: Balanced slate navy for secondary body text on light backgrounds; switches to **Starlight Slate (`#C9D2DD`)** across deep navy surfaces.
- **Maritime Gold (`#F4B63F`)**: Rich brass gold reserved for high-converting focal triggers: primary transactional buttons, package highlights, key metric counters, and fold accents.
- **Urgency Coral (`#FF6B57`)**: Specialized discount pill badge color; always pairs with dark navy typography (`#0B1D33`) to maintain accessible contrast.
- **Direct WhatsApp Hook (`#25D366`)**: Universal conversational commerce anchor; always executed with dark navy text (`#0B1D33`) for structural unity.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages structural geometry counterbalanced by humanistic rhythm and classical literary flair:

1. **Display & Headings (Poppins SemiBold / Bold)**: Provides geometric clarity, commercial solidity, and absolute authority on package pricing and conversion arguments. Numbers in Poppins command trust and focus.
2. **Body & Utility (Inter Regular / Medium)**: Delivers unmatched technical legibility for Colombian Spanish copy, ensuring flawless character spacing across accents, tildes, and inverted punctuation marks.
3. **Editorial Accent (Lora Italic SemiBold)**: Applied strictly to 1–3 chosen words in hero titles and closing calls to action (e.g., *“la vela que tu marca necesita”*). Displayed in Gold (`#F4B63F`) against Midnight Navy fields (`#0B1D33`), introducing an unmistakable artisanal signature.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an **8px strict geometric system** arranged within an adaptable 12-column responsive grid:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, max-width content container set to 1240px, framed with 32px (`2rem`) page safe margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, dynamic fluid padding across canvas borders.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, with 20px (`1.25rem`) viewport outer margins.

### Section Pacing & Band Transitions
Full-bleed sections alternate deliberately between **Paper Cream (`#FBF7EF`)** and **Midnight Navy (`#0B1D33`)**. The transition boundary between light and navy zones features a customized golden wavy maritime stroke separator (`height: 32px`, fill: `#F4B63F` or wave cut line) reinforcing the nautical sail concept.

## Elevation & Depth

In line with the warm tactile paper ethos, elevation avoids synthetic deep black drop-shadows, prioritizing tactile paper layering and brass reflections.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Flush paper stock (`#FBF7EF` or navy bands `#0B1D33`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` card body with a structural 1px perimeter outline in `#E6DCC8`. Underpinned by a warm, diffuse ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 29, 51, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive / Hover)**: Physical page lift effect: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(11, 29, 51, 0.10)`, with card translation `translateY(-4px)` to simulate lifting deck paper.
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Drawers)**: High-utility floating navigation and checkout triggers: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(11, 29, 51, 0.16)`.
- **The Fold Motif**: The top-right corner of flagship cards utilizes a decorative CSS/SVG clipped folded corner (dog-ear fold), revealing an exposed triangular underside styled with Maritime Gold (`#F4B63F`) and an inner soft crease shadow.

## Shapes

The form language combines structured geometry with organic softness:

- **Standard Buttons**: Curated radius of `10px` for balanced tactility and clickable intent.
- **Package Cards & Panels**: Engineered with `16px` outer corner curvature on bottom and left edges, with the specialized 24px folded geometry on the top-right apex.
- **Pill Badges**: Full radius (`9999px`) for discount tags, category chips, and live status signals.
- **Inputs & Fields**: `10px` radius matching the primary button language for visual balance across checkout and consultation forms.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Transactional (E-Store Checkout / Comprar Ahora)**: Background `#F4B63F`, text `#0B1D33` (Poppins SemiBold), radius 10px, padding 14px 28px. On hover: background `#E5A832` with subtle tactile downward shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline (Ver Detalles / Portafolio)**: 1.5px solid border `#0B1D33`, transparent background, text `#0B1D33`. On dark bands: border `#F4B63F`, text `#F4B63F`.
- **WhatsApp Commercial Anchor**: Background `#25D366`, typography `#0B1D33` (Inter SemiBold), accompanied by the 2px rounded stroke WhatsApp emblem.

### 2. Website Package Cards (The Sail Cards)
- **Container**: Solid `#FFFFFF` interior, 1px border in `#E6DCC8`, radius 16px.
- **Fold Detail**: Top-right corner styled with a 24px angled fold line displaying an inner `#F4B63F` accent corner.
- **Featured / Best-Seller Tier**: Upgraded perimeter border (2px `#F4B63F`), accompanied by a floating pill badge at top center reading *"MÁS VENDIDO"* in `#0B1D33` over `#F4B63F`.
- **Pricing Display**: Poppins Bold 44px (`#0B1D33`), clear Colombian Peso currency tags (`COP $X.XXX.XXX`), and breakdown of delivery velocity (e.g., *"Entrega en 5 días hábiles"*).

### 3. Badges & Chips
- **Discount & Promo Badges**: Fill `#FF6B57`, typography `#0B1D33` (Inter Bold 12px uppercase).
- **Capability Chips**: Background `#EFE5D2`, text `#0B1D33` (Inter Medium 13px), radius 9999px, padding 6px 14px.

### 4. Iconography
- **Style**: 2px rounded line strokes with consistent rounded joins and caps.
- **Palette**: Rendered in Maritime Gold (`#F4B63F`) inside navy modules, or Deep Navy (`#0B1D33`) inside light modules. Themes honor nautical navigation: compasses, sextants, sails, anchors, and speed-wind trajectories.

### 5. Section Dividers
- **Nautical Wave (Separador de Ola)**: Custom horizontal vector wave rendered in `#F4B63F` or transitioning between `#FBF7EF` and `#0B1D33` to separate narrative blocks seamlessly.

### 6. Colombian Localized Copy Guidelines
- Voice: Direct, highly polished, commercial, and solution-focused.
- Key Nomenclature: *“Paquetes Web Todo Incluido”*, *“Lanza tu web en tiempo récord”*, *“Diseño enfocado en ventas”*, *“Sin enredos técnicos”*.
- Action verbs favor direct momentum: *“Elegir Paquete”*, *“Hablar con un Asesor”*, *“Ver Demo en Vivo”*.
